## 6. What these numbers do and do not show

- All noise is synthetic or from public recordings; speech leakage and the reference-mic geometry are simulated, not measured on the real headset. Real-hardware validation is a separate step.
- One training seed per configuration except where a second seed is listed; the seed-to-seed row above is the yardstick for how large a difference must be to mean anything.
- PESQ/SI-SNR benefit partly from the dereverberating target (direct sound + 5 ms).
- Energy-based SNR overstates how easy impulsive noise is; the weapon set (level set by peak SPL) is the harder test.
- INT8 and Raspberry Pi timing are reported separately (they need the device).
